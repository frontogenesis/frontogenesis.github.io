Forecast Summary

Updated July 31, 2026

Southwest Louisiana: Light southwesterly winds and low seas will continue over Southwest Louisiana coastal waters through Saturday. A weak front early Sunday will bring west-northwesterly winds of 15-20 knots along with a 30-70% chance of showers and thunderstorms, followed by a return to light southerly winds and seas Monday onward. Near the coast, dangerous heat with afternoon highs in the mid to upper 90s and heat indices of 102-109°F will persist through Saturday before a slight drop in humidity Sunday; afternoon thunderstorms are possible each day next week with highs remaining in the low to mid 90s.

Texas Coastal Bend: A hot and mostly dry weekend is expected along the Texas Coastal Bend, with only a slight chance of showers or storms Sunday. Near the coast, gentle to moderate onshore winds will bring occasional fresh gusts, especially overnight and in the morning, leading to Small Craft Advisory conditions at times over the bays and nearshore waters south of Port Aransas. Seas will generally run 2 to 4 feet with no significant marine hazards anticipated and rain chances remaining very low.
